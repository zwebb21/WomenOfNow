Mrs. Donna McGowan

Founder and Visionary Entrepreneur

Donna McGowan is a woman, wife, mother, speaker, and founder of Women of NOW LLC. Her vision centers on unity, strength, and whole-woman leadership for women in business, ministry, and everyday life.

Donna leads women to walk in the power revealed within them. In her words, "It's a win-win."

What You Will Learn in 7 Weeks of Courage

Creation

Genesis 1-2: Examine the roles of men and women in God's original design.

Eve's Creation and Temptation

Genesis 2-3: Discuss Eve's role and the consequences of disobedience.

Sarah's Faith

Genesis 18:1-15; 21:1-7: Explore Sarah's journey of faith and motherhood.

Hannah's Prayer

1 Samuel 1-2: Reflect on Hannah's prayer and God's faithfulness.

Esther's Courage

Esther: Study Esther's bravery in saving her people.

Ruth's Loyalty

Ruth: Consider Ruth's loyalty and devotion to Naomi and Boaz.

Mary's Magnificat

Luke 1:46-55: Reflect on Mary's song of praise and humility.

Mary and Martha

Luke 10:38-42: Consider the balance between serving and spending time with Jesus.
